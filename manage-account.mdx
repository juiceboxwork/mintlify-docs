## Account settings

To manage your Juicebox account, select your name or organization at the bottom of the left-hand menu, then select ‘Account Settings.’ This opens up your account menu, which is organized into three main sections:

- **Personal**
- **Organization**
- **Data & sourcing**

![Screenshot 2026-07-07 at 3.29.06 PM](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juiceboxwork/docs/kb-migration/images/manage-account/Screenshot_2026-07-07_at_3-29-06_PM.png)

## Personal

### Mailbox and emails

Here, you can connect or switch your mailbox, add a custom signature, and enable candidate reply notifications. Juicebox sends these updates to your inbox and includes the candidate’s profile (not visible to the candidate).

You can also enable automated reminders, link tracking, or limit outreach to verified domains only, which reduces [bounce rates](/maximize-replies).

![Screenshot 2026-07-07 at 3.28.10 PM](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juiceboxwork/docs/kb-migration/images/manage-account/Screenshot_2026-07-07_at_3-28-10_PM.png)

### Email signatures

Selecting **Manage Signatures** takes you to the **Integrations** tab. From there, you can:

- Add new signatures
- Edit or duplicate existing ones
- Assign a signature to a mailbox
- Delete a signature

Any changes will apply to all mailboxes and active sequences using that signature.

## Organization

### General

View and update organizational settings. These settings affect all users in the workspace. Organization settings include:

- Organization name
- Organization logo
- SSO configuration (available on the business plan)
- Allow single exports
- Allow bulk exports
- Only export with contact info
- Add notes for Stack Rank and Agents
- Multiple active sequences per candidate
- Auto-enroll with fallback after email bounce
- Force include unsubscribe link
- Enforced footer addendum
- Enforce Email strict mode (locks Email strict mode on for every user in the organization)
- Available email preferences
- Organization email sending limit
- Monthly usage reports (available on the business plan)

### Agents

Manage existing agent limits or add new agents to your organization from this section. [_Learn more about what agents can do in your workflows._](/juicebox-agents)

#### Recent ATS activity window

The **Recent ATS activity window** setting under **Agent controls** defines which ATS candidates count as recent for your whole organization. Only admins can change it. Choose **Any time** to turn it off, or pick 90, 180, 270, or 365 days.

A candidate counts as recent when their ATS application is Active, or is Archived or Hired with a last interaction inside the window. When you set a window:

- The [Exclude recent ATS candidates](/talent-rediscovery#exclude-recent-ats-candidates) search filter becomes available.
- Agents that exclude ATS profiles across all stages exclude only recent ATS candidates. Older ATS candidates stay in the Agent's pool.
- Agents warn you about an ATS match before outreach only when that candidate is recent.

The setting is unavailable if your ATS doesn't sync application activity to Juicebox. In that case, Juicebox keeps excluding everyone your ATS knows about.

## Data & sourcing

### **DNC companies (do not contact company settings)**

Add companies to your organization's DNC list to exclude contacts working at selected companies from email sequences and search results.

_Auto exclude_ is on by default. It automatically filters out profiles from these companies in new searches, Agents, intakes, and searches started from Slack for the entire team. Admins can turn off **Auto exclude DNC companies in searches** to disable it for the organization. You can still turn off exclusion for an individual search. [_Learn more about DNC enforcement._](/dnc#dnc-enforcement)

![Screenshot 2026-07-07 at 4.38.44 PM](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juiceboxwork/docs/kb-migration/images/manage-account/Screenshot_2026-07-07_at_4-38-44_PM.png)

### **DNC countries (do not contact country settings)**

Add selected countries to exclude them from email sequences and search results. Country auto exclude is off by default. Enable it to automatically filter out profiles from these countries for the entire team.

Admins can also add specific people as exceptions to the company and country DNC lists. [_Learn more about contact-level DNC overrides._](/dnc#3-contact-level-dnc-overrides)

![Screenshot 2026-07-07 at 4.40.10 PM](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juiceboxwork/docs/kb-migration/images/manage-account/Screenshot_2026-07-07_at_4-40-10_PM.png)

### Presets

Manage reusable preset lists for search filters here. Presets let you save and reuse commonly used search criteria so you can launch searches faster and keep requirements consistent across roles. Use Juicebox’s pre-built presets or create your own custom presets for the types of candidates your team searches for most often.

![Screenshot 2026-07-07 at 4.41.29 PM](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juiceboxwork/docs/kb-migration/images/manage-account/Screenshot_2026-07-07_at_4-41-29_PM.png)

### ATS

Use this page to keep Juicebox projects, Agents, and ATS workflows aligned.⁠ ATS Settings let admins manage their ATS connection, configure automated exports, and control how candidates move from Juicebox into the right ATS job or stage. ATS integration is available on the business plan.

![Screenshot 2026-07-07 at 4.41.47 PM](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juiceboxwork/docs/kb-migration/images/manage-account/Screenshot_2026-07-07_at_4-41-47_PM.png)

### Memory

Manage the preferences and guidance Juicebox uses to personalize AI-generated work across your workspace. Memory Settings help Juicebox remember your team’s preferences, feedback, and instructions so future searches, Agents, and AI-generated outreach are more aligned with how your team works.
