## Overview

An **intake** is the hiring-manager brief attached to a project — the notes, must-haves, and context that shape how you source for the role. In Juicebox, an intake is more than a notes document:

- A rich-text notes editor with debounced autosave, so you can capture the kickoff call in real time.
- An optional **Juicebox notetaker** that joins your calendar meeting (or an ad-hoc meeting URL), records it, and streams a live transcript into the intake.
- A post-call **AI Summary** that reformats the transcript into the structured sections your intake template expects.

Each project has one intake, and the intake title defaults to the project name so it's easy to find later.

## Where to find your intake

Once the feature is enabled for your org, you'll see a new **Intake** entry in the left-hand project sidebar, marked with a `New` badge:

- If the project doesn't have an intake yet, the sidebar shows **+ Create Intake**.
- If an intake already exists, the sidebar links straight to it.

Selecting either option takes you to the intake page for the current project.

## Creating an intake

Clicking **+ Create Intake** opens the **Add Intake** dialog, which offers three starting points:

- **From scratch.** Start with an empty document — useful if you don't have a template yet or want to freeform the call.
- **Clone an existing intake.** Copy the notes, template, and structure from another intake in your org. Great when you're hiring for another role on the same team and want to reuse the same brief format.
- **From a template.** Pick from your organization's shared intake templates (or your personal templates). Templates define the section headings and prompts that the post-call AI Summary will fill in.

The dialog previews the selected source before you create the intake, so you can confirm you've picked the right template or intake to clone. The new intake's title defaults to the project name — rename it any time from the intake page.

## Intake templates

Templates are reusable intake structures — the section headings and sub-fields (things like "Must-haves," "Nice-to-haves," "Team context," "Compensation") that every intake for that role type should capture.

Open the **Templates** manager from the intake page to create, edit, share, or delete templates. Each template can be:

- **Personal** — visible only to you.
- **Shared with the organization** — anyone on your team can pick it when creating an intake.

### Import from a Google Doc

If your team already has an intake questionnaire in Google Docs, use **Import from Google Doc** in the templates manager. Paste the shareable link and Juicebox will:

1. Fetch the document contents.
2. Run an **AI Reformat** pass that cleans up formatting, promotes section titles to headings, and bolds sub-field labels so the post-call AI Summary can populate them.
3. Drop the reformatted template into the editor for you to review before saving.

You can also click **Reformat with AI** at any time on an existing template to re-run the same pass — helpful after pasting in raw text.

<Tip>
  For the AI Summary to fill in a section correctly, keep each section title as a heading and each sub-field as a **bold** label followed by a description. The **Reformat with AI** action does this for you automatically.
</Tip>

## The intake page

The intake page has three tabs at the top:

- **Notes** — the rich-text editor where you write the intake by hand.
- **AI Summary** — the structured summary generated from the meeting transcript, laid out according to the selected template's sections.
- **Transcript** — the meeting transcript, updated live while the notetaker is in the call and finalized after the call ends.

### Notes

The Notes tab is a full Tiptap rich-text editor: headings, lists, bold, italic, and links. Changes autosave with a short debounce, and a **Saved** indicator confirms the latest keystrokes have persisted. There's no manual save button — just keep typing.

You can also change the template from the Notes tab. Swapping templates preserves your existing notes and only changes the sections that the AI Summary will produce.

### AI Summary

The AI Summary is generated automatically after a notetaker session ends (see [Recording a meeting](#recording-a-meeting)). Juicebox reads the transcript, splits it by the sections defined in your template, and fills in each sub-field with the relevant content from the call.

If the notetaker joins a follow-up session on the same intake, the AI Summary is re-run against the combined transcript so it always reflects the full conversation to date.

### Transcript

The Transcript tab shows the live speaker-attributed transcript while the notetaker is in the meeting, and the finalized transcript after the call ends. When you send the notetaker into a follow-up meeting on the same intake, the new session is appended to the existing transcript instead of replacing it.

### Find in page

Use `⌘F` (macOS) or `Ctrl+F` (Windows/Linux) on the intake page to open find-in-page. The search box highlights every match across the active tab, and you can cycle forward and backward through matches to jump between them. Press `Esc` to close.

## Recording a meeting

The Juicebox notetaker is a bot (powered by [Recall.ai](https://recall.ai)) that joins a video call, records it, and streams the transcript back into the intake in real time. Deepgram is the default transcription provider.

### Send the notetaker to a meeting

From the intake page, click **Send notetaker**. You have two options:

- **Upcoming meeting.** Juicebox lists the meetings from your connected calendar for the next several hours. Pick one and Juicebox schedules the bot to join at the meeting's start time.
- **Ad-hoc meeting URL.** Paste a Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams link and the bot joins right now.

The notetaker supports the video providers Recall.ai supports (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams). Once dispatched, the bot's status appears on the intake page: `scheduled`, `joining`, `in call`, `recording`, `done`, or `failed`.

<Note>
  Live streaming of the transcript is required for the bot to launch — Juicebox uses the live transcript to populate the Transcript tab in real time. If your organization's Recall.ai configuration disables live transcription, the bot will refuse to start.
</Note>

### While the bot is in the call

- The **Transcript** tab fills in live as the bot transcribes the meeting.
- The Notes tab remains fully editable — you can take manual notes while the bot records.
- Closing the browser tab does not affect the bot; it continues recording until the meeting ends.

### After the call

Once the meeting ends:

1. The transcript is finalized in the Transcript tab.
2. Juicebox runs the post-call AI pipeline, which reads the transcript, walks the template section by section, and populates each sub-field of the **AI Summary**.
3. Both the Notes tab (your manual notes) and the AI Summary tab (Juicebox's structured recap) are available side by side.

### Follow-up sessions

If a single intake spans multiple meetings — for example, a kickoff call followed by a calibration sync — click **Send notetaker** again from the same intake. The next session's transcript is **appended** to the existing transcript, and the AI Summary is re-run against the combined content so the summary always reflects the whole conversation.

## Requirements

To use intakes and the notetaker:

- The `project-intake` early-access flag must be enabled for your org.
- To send the notetaker to an upcoming meeting, you need a connected Google or Outlook calendar (the same connection used by [Smart Notes](/smart-notes) and [Email Integrations](/email-integrations)).
- Allow microphone permissions in your browser on the intake page if you want to use browser-side transcription features.
