title: "Hire For Good"
---

Hire for Good is our way of celebrating the hires you make through Juicebox. When you report a hire sourced on the platform, Juicebox donates \$50 to a charity you select from our curated list. There's no cost to you, and it only takes a minute to submit.

## Who can participate

Hire for Good is available to customers with an active paid subscription (self-serve or Business plans) whose accounts are in good standing.

## How to submit a hire

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Hire for Good flow">
    You can start a submission from the home page or a project page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify the candidate you hired">
    Select the candidate from your account. If the candidate's profile no longer exists in Juicebox, you can type in their name manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a project (optional)">
    You can associate the hire with one of your projects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a charity">
    Pick one charity from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    You'll see a confirmation, and your submission enters review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each hire can only be submitted once, and there's currently no deadline for reporting a hire.

## Tracking your submission

You can check the status of your submissions in **Settings**. Submissions move through two statuses:

- **In review**: We're verifying the hire against our platform data. Review can take up to 60 days.
- **Validated**: The hire is approved and the donation will be made.

Juicebox aggregates approved donations and sends them to charities quarterly.

## Choosing a charity

You can select any charity from Juicebox's current list, which includes causes like expanding access to computer science education, medical relief, and food assistance. The list may change over time. If a charity you selected can no longer accept a donation, we may direct it to a comparable charity on the list or ask you to make a new selection.

## Good to know

- Donations are made by Juicebox, in Juicebox's name, from Juicebox's own funds. No funds pass through you, and donations are not tax deductible for you.
- Donations are not a credit, discount, or refund, and can't be exchanged for cash or anything else of value.
- All submissions are reviewed, and Juicebox may decline submissions that appear inaccurate, duplicative, or inconsistent with platform data.
- By submitting, you confirm the information is accurate, the hire was sourced through Juicebox, and you have any consents needed to share the candidate's information.
- Juicebox may change the donation amount, charity list, or program at any time. Approved submissions are honored even if the program changes afterward.

For full details, see the [Hire for Good Program Terms](https://juicebox.ai/terms/hire-for-good).
