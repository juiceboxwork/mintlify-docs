## Overview

Recruiters and hiring managers already coordinate on roles in Slack, but candidate profiles live in other tabs.

Juicebox for Slack closes that gap: connect a Project or Agent to the channel where the team works, and updates show up at the right time — right in the thread, ready to act on.

Juicebox for Slack lets you:

- **Notify, answer, and act** on existing projects, sequences, and Agents
- **Review and surface leads** produced by an existing Agent or shortlist
- **Act faster** on common actions — especially on mobile

## Set up in three steps

<Note>
  A paid Slack plan is required to use the Juicebox AI agent in the Slack app container. All other Juicebox for Slack features work on free Slack plans, including channel notifications, lead review carousels, and `@Juicebox` mentions in channels and threads.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Slack to Juicebox">
    Navigate to the **Integrations** tab and connect the Slack integration to Juicebox. This creates a single workspace connection.

    Each Juicebox organization connects to exactly one Slack workspace, and each Slack workspace connects to exactly one Juicebox organization. Juicebox blocks a connect that would replace an existing pairing. This happens when the workspace already belongs to another Juicebox organization, or when your organization is already connected to a different workspace. Disconnect the existing pairing first, then try again. Re-authorizing the same organization and workspace still works and is safe to run to refresh permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a project or Agent">
    From the **Projects** page, click the project's **Slack** row to open the Slack dialog, then pick a channel from the searchable picker. For a channel you aren't in, or a private channel, paste its channel link or ID into the picker instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag @Juicebox in the Slack channel">
    Get started by asking Juicebox what it can do in Slack.

    Type:

    ```text
    @Juicebox, what can you do?
    ```

    Juicebox is added to the channel and starts surfacing notifications, project updates, and shortlist review requests there. For a private channel, invite `@Juicebox` to the channel first so it can reply to mentions and thread follow-ups.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Core capabilities

### Review leads directly in Slack channels

When a review is requested, Slack posts a candidate carousel: approve, skip, or reject each profile.

- Add context during the decision; feedback syncs back and informs the Agent's learning and the platform.
- Carousels hold up to 10 cards, and press **See More** to generate more profiles at the end of the carousel.
- Shortlist reviews and Agent leads follow the same flow.

### Get project and sequence notifications

Once a Project is connected to a Slack channel, activity shows up in that channel:

- New Agent leads ready for review, or interested replies from an email sequence
- Project activity such as name changes or collaborators being added or removed
- Project activity summaries on a configurable cadence

You can also chat directly with Juicebox in Slack to get project notifications and status updates.

### Ask @Juicebox for project and Agent analytics

Tag `@Juicebox` in your Slack Chat to get quick answers without navigating dashboards:

- Pending, approved, or reviewed lead counts at a glance
- Outreach volume, such as how many people were contacted or added to a sequence
- Pipeline, project, Agent, or sequence health, answered in the thread
- What happened to a profile after it was approved

<Note>
  Juicebox uses LLMs that have the potential to generate inaccurate results.
</Note>

#### What Slack analytics can answer on your plan

Slack mirrors the same analytics access rules as the Juicebox web app, so what `@Juicebox` will answer depends on your plan and role.

**Open on every paid Juicebox plan (any Juicebox member in the channel can ask):**

- A project's pipeline by stage, candidate lists, and shortlist activity
- Agent health listings, such as which Agents are paused or out of leads
- Project-scoped counts, such as how many candidates were shortlisted for a specific project
- All outreach analytics, including emails sent per teammate and sequence performance

**Juicebox Business plan and analytics access required:**

- Searches run per recruiter and profiles viewed
- Per-teammate leaderboards, such as who shortlisted the most candidates
- Cross-project shortlist rollups, such as shortlisted candidates per project without naming a project
- Org-wide project and Agent counts, such as how many Agents you have

To ask these usage questions in Slack, you need a Juicebox Business plan and one of the following roles: admin, read-only admin, or the `analyticsAdmins` role granted per member from **Settings → Team members**.

### Manage notifications from Slack

Configure which Juicebox notifications a channel receives without leaving Slack. In any channel connected to a project or Agent, run:

```text
@Juicebox settings
```

Juicebox posts a launcher that opens an in-Slack configuration modal for the projects and Agents linked to that channel. From the modal you can:

- **Mute all notifications** for the project or Agent in this channel
- **Choose which notification groups fire** — for example, new leads, sequence replies, or project activity

Changes made in Slack stay in sync with the notification settings on the web, so you can manage the same connection from either surface. Muting keeps your group selections intact, so unmuting restores your previous choices. The project activity summary schedule (daily, weekly, or monthly digest) is unchanged and continues to follow the cadence set in Juicebox.

Before the modal opens or a change is saved, Juicebox checks that your Slack account is linked to a Juicebox user and that you have permission to manage the project or Agent. If you don't, Juicebox posts a private message in the channel explaining why instead of opening the modal.

### Take profile actions and create tasks

Manage profiles and follow-up straight from Slack:

- Add a **note** or update **tags** on a profile
- Create a **reminder or task** and assign it to a teammate in the channel — the assignee is tagged in Slack, and the task and note live in Juicebox
- Share a **LinkedIn URL** and add it straight to a project shortlist

### Work with saved searches from Slack

Ask `@Juicebox` to browse, review, create, or edit the saved searches inside a project without leaving Slack. These tools operate on ordinary saved searches; Agent configuration and recalibration stay on the Agent tools.

**Browse and review (read-only)**

Available to any licensed Juicebox user connected to Slack.

- **List searches** — show the saved searches in a project, optionally filtered by a title fragment. Returns each search's title, execution state, last-updated timestamp, and Juicebox link.
- **Inspect a search** — read one saved search's filter summary, criteria, execution state, and canonical link. Accepts a search ID, exact or partial title, or a canonical Juicebox saved-search URL. Ambiguous titles return candidates so you can confirm which one you meant.
- **See profiles** — return up to five candidates from the most recent completed run of a saved search. This never re-runs the search or marks profiles as viewed. If no run has finished yet, Juicebox says the results aren't cached instead of executing one.

**Create and update**

Available to regular and admin licensed seats on non-Agent projects. Hiring-manager, sequence, read-only-admin, and guest accounts get read-only access at most.

- **Create search** — paste a role brief or job description. Juicebox builds filters and criteria, saves the search in the project you name, and runs it immediately. The response includes the search link, pool size, first-page count, and up to five candidates. If the source looks like a job description, Juicebox uses JD mode automatically.
- **Update search** — apply a natural-language edit to one existing saved search and re-run it.

Example prompts:

```text
@juicebox, list saved searches in [Project].
@juicebox, show me the filters on the "Staff PM - NYC" search.
@juicebox, create a saved search in [Project] for a senior backend engineer with Go and Kubernetes based in the EU.
@juicebox, in [Project], update "Staff PM - NYC" to also include product-led-growth experience.
```

Saved-search writes are gated on licensed-seat projects.

### Channel reachability

If a linked channel needs Juicebox to be invited before it can reply to mentions and thread follow-ups, the Slack dialog says so under the channel picker. Run `/invite @Juicebox` in the channel, then click the refresh icon next to the picker.

If the channel is archived, the dialog surfaces that instead and asks you to pick another channel. See [Archived Slack channels](#archived-slack-channels).

### Disconnect a project from its Slack channel

If a project was linked to the wrong Slack channel, or you no longer want notifications for it in Slack, you can unlink it from the project's configuration without leaving the flow.

1. Open the project and click the **Slack** row that shows the connected channel name.
2. In the dialog, click **Disconnect** next to the channel name.
3. Confirm **Disconnect** in the prompt.

Notifications stop immediately, and Slack guests in that channel lose the ability to ask `@Juicebox` about this project. Disconnecting clears your notification settings for this project, so if you reconnect the project to a channel later, notifications start from the defaults.

To disconnect the entire workspace instead of a single project:

1. Go to **Integrations**.
2. On the Slack card, click **Manage connections**.
3. In the dialog, click **Options** > **Remove all connections**.
4. Confirm **Remove all connections** in the prompt.

This removes the Slack integration from your organization and disconnects it from every project and Agent, so a different workspace can be connected. You can reconnect Slack at any time.

### Archived Slack channels

If a Slack channel linked to a Juicebox project or Agent is archived, Juicebox marks the connection inactive and stops sending notifications to that channel. Your notification preferences and the connection record are preserved. To resume delivery with the same settings, reconnect the project or Agent to a channel from the **Projects** page.

### Slack guests

A Slack guest is a channel member Juicebox can't match to a licensed Juicebox user. Guests can see Juicebox messages in the channel and ask project-scoped questions about the channels they're in, but can't run cross-project or usage analytics.

Guests can:

- Read notifications, review requests, and replies Juicebox posts in the channel
- Ask `@Juicebox` for pipeline, candidate lists, Agent health, and outreach questions about the projects their channel is linked to. Juicebox automatically scopes the answer to those projects.
- Ask general questions about how Juicebox works (for example, "how does Juicebox work?")

Guests can't:

- Ask `@Juicebox` for usage analytics such as searches run, profiles viewed, per-teammate leaderboards, or org-wide project and Agent counts
- Take profile actions, add to shortlists, or create tasks

When a guest asks a question that would require a Juicebox seat, Juicebox replies with a guest-specific message that names the missing membership and offers to have someone invite them to Juicebox. The reply also points at the `@Juicebox account` command, so a teammate whose Slack email doesn't match their Juicebox email can connect the two themselves.

### Connect a Slack identity to a Juicebox account

Juicebox matches Slack members to Juicebox users by email. If a teammate's Slack email doesn't match the email on their Juicebox account, they can connect the two identities themselves without an admin.

When Juicebox can't match a Slack user by email, it sends them a private message only they can see, with a **Sign in to Juicebox** button. Anyone can also trigger the same message on demand by typing `@Juicebox account` (or `@Juicebox find my account`) in any channel where Juicebox is present.

After clicking the button, they sign in to Juicebox, confirm which Slack identity and Juicebox account will be connected, and finish the link. Juicebox then treats the two identities as the same person for permissions, assignments, and notifications.

If they're already linked, `@Juicebox account` confirms who they're connected as. The link only works within your Juicebox organization.

## Slack permissions requested

When you connect Slack, Juicebox requests the following scopes:

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `app_mentions:read` | Detect when a user starts a request with @Juicebox, so Juicebox knows when to respond. |
| `assistant:write` | Show Juicebox as a Slack AI assistant and show progress while it processes a request. |
| `channels:history` | Read messages in public channels where Juicebox is present, so it can understand and answer requests. |
| `channels:read` | Identify public channels, so Juicebox can maintain the correct project-to-channel connection. |
| `channels:manage` | Create a public channel when a user chooses to create one for a Juicebox project or Agent. |
| `channels:write.invites` | Invite selected project members to a public channel that Juicebox creates. |
| `chat:write` | Send replies, notifications, and review requests as Juicebox. |
| `chat:write.public` | Send a message to a connected public channel when Juicebox is not yet a channel member. |
| `files:read` | Read supported files shared where Juicebox is present, so it can use them when answering a request. |
| `files:write` | Upload files or images that Juicebox includes in a Slack response. |
| `groups:history` | Read messages in private channels where Juicebox is present, so it can understand and answer requests. |
| `groups:read` | Identify private channels where Juicebox is present, so it can maintain the correct project-to-channel connection. |
| `groups:write` | Create a private channel for a confidential Juicebox project or Agent. |
| `groups:write.invites` | Invite selected project members to a private channel that Juicebox creates. |
| `im:history` | Read direct messages with Juicebox, so it can understand the conversation and answer follow-up questions. |
| `im:read` | Identify direct-message conversations with Juicebox, so it can reply in the correct conversation. |
| `im:write` | Start a direct message when Juicebox must send a private response. |
| `links:read` | Detect Juicebox links in messages, so it can determine whether a preview is available. |
| `links:write` | Show a preview for supported Juicebox links. |
| `mpim:history` | Read group direct messages where Juicebox is present, so it can understand and answer requests. |
| `mpim:read` | Identify group direct messages where Juicebox is present, so it can reply in the correct conversation. |
| `reactions:read` | Read reactions on Juicebox messages, so it can recognize existing feedback or status. |
| `reactions:write` | Add or remove a reaction to acknowledge a request or show its status. |
| `users:read` | Identify Slack members, so Juicebox can use the correct names, mentions, assignments, and invitations. |
| `users:read.email` | Match a Slack member to the correct Juicebox user for access checks, assignments, and invitations. |

## Best practices

- The integration creates one workspace connection, but supports unlimited Slack channel to project or Agent mappings. We recommend **one channel per project or Agent**, though some teams put everything in a single channel.
- Notifications are scoped to the channel — anyone in the channel gets them. Some notifications (project renamed, collaborators added or removed) can't be disabled. The project activity summary has a configurable cadence — it defaults to weekly, and can be set to daily or monthly.
- You only need to tag `@Juicebox` when starting a new thread, not on every message within the thread.
- DMing Juicebox directly works for status and analytics questions, and works for users without a Juicebox seat (view-only). Lead review cannot happen in DMs.

  <Accordion title="Can anyone in my Slack workspace connect Juicebox to a channel?">

    Once the workspace is connected, anyone with access to a project can link it to a Slack channel from the **Projects** page.

    Any Slack member of a channel can add the bot to that channel by tagging `@Juicebox` or running `/invite @Juicebox`. If your Slack workspace restricts who can add apps to channels, that restriction applies here too.

  <Accordion title="How does the integration collect, manage, and store candidate data?">
    Juicebox for Slack surfaces candidate data that already lives in your Juicebox organization. It does not collect candidate data from Slack, and it does not use messages in your Slack workspace to build the Juicebox candidate database.

    For details on how Juicebox handles data, see the [Juicebox privacy policy](https://juicebox.ai/privacy-policy) and the [Data Sources](/data-sources) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Slack notifications aren't showing up in my channel after installing this integration">
    Check each of the following:

    1. The project or Agent is linked to that specific channel. Open the project and look for the **Slack** row — it should show the channel name.
    2. Juicebox has been added to the channel. Tag `@Juicebox` or run `/invite @Juicebox` in the channel to add it. See [Channel reachability](#channel-reachability) if the project's Slack row asks you to invite Juicebox.
    3. Notifications for the project aren't muted. Run `@Juicebox settings` in the channel and confirm the relevant notification groups are on.
    4. The channel hasn't been archived. Archiving a linked channel marks the connection inactive and stops delivery.

    If notifications still don't arrive, email [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why doesn't the candidate preview in Slack match what I see in Juicebox?">
    Slack cards show a compact summary of a candidate. The full profile — including all criteria, sources, and history — lives in Juicebox. Open the profile link in the Slack card to see everything Juicebox has on that candidate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I restrict which channels the Juicebox bot is allowed to post in?">
    Juicebox only posts in a channel after it has been added to that channel and a project or Agent has been linked to it. To stop Juicebox from posting in a specific channel, either disconnect the project from that channel or remove Juicebox from the channel in Slack.

    To disconnect a project from its channel:

    1. Open the project and click the **Slack** row that shows the connected channel name.
    2. In the dialog, click **Disconnect** next to the channel name.
    3. Confirm **Disconnect** in the prompt.

    To remove Juicebox from a channel entirely, use Slack's **Remove app** action on the channel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What OAuth scopes does Juicebox request, and can I limit them?">
    See [Slack permissions requested](#slack-permissions-requested) above for the full list of scopes and what each one is used for. All listed scopes are required for the integration to work as documented — you can't remove individual scopes during install.

    If your organization needs a narrower permission set, email [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai) to discuss what's possible.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
