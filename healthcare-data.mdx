Juicebox is piloting healthcare data for millions of US healthcare professionals. You can search by clinical credentials, review each provider's clinical background on their profile, and run outreach without leaving Juicebox.

This includes providers who may not appear in traditional searches, such as clinicians with no LinkedIn profile.

## What you can do

- **Search millions of US healthcare professionals**, including providers who never showed up in your searches before.
- **Filter on the clinical credentials that qualify them**, such as state license, specialty, board certification, training, and hospital privileges.
- **Reach out knowing their practice.** Every profile carries the clinical record, so your message lands on their specialty.

## How to search

Describe who you are looking for in plain language. For the best results, name the role, the state, and the credential that decides the hire. Start broad, review the pool, then narrow.

Example searches:

- "Physicians certified in family medicine, licensed in Texas, with experience in senior living or value-based primary care"
- "Dentists licensed in Arizona who are in private practice"
- "Nurse practitioners licensed in Florida who work in cardiology"

Juicebox turns your search into filters and ranking criteria automatically. You can review and adjust them any time.

## Filter on clinical credentials

Open the **Healthcare** filter group in Search to filter on:

| Filter | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Role** | RN, Nurse Practitioner, Physician |
| **Licensed in** | Texas, California, New York |
| **Practicing in** | Los Angeles, Rochester, Minnesota |
| **Specialty & training** | Emergency Medicine, Oncology, Cardiology Fellowship |
| **Subspecialty** | Pediatric Neurosurgery, Interventional Radiology |
| **Clinical credentials** | Board certified, ACLS, CCRN |
| **Affiliated with** | Mayo Clinic, UCSF, Cleveland Clinic |
| **Requirements** | Hospital privileges, private practice background, travel or contract background |

<Note>
  **Licensed in** and **Practicing in** are different filters. Licensed in is where a provider holds a state license to practice. Practicing in is where they actually work. A provider can be licensed in a state where they do not currently practice.
</Note>

## Review the healthcare profile

Every healthcare professional's record includes a **Healthcare** tab with a birds eye view of their clinical background:

- Specialty
- Credentials and certifications
- Specialties and training
- Practice authorization, including license status and expiration
- Practice relationships, such as hospital privileges

## Reach out from Juicebox

Once you have found the right providers, add them to a shortlist or a sequence directly from the search results. Because each profile carries the clinical record, you can tailor your outreach to their specialty without switching tools.

## Who this is for

- **Health systems and hospitals** hiring clinical staff against license and privilege requirements
- **Digital health, Pharma, Biotech, and Medical device companies** hiring clinicians, medical affairs, and regulatory roles
- **Healthcare staffing and recruiting firms** placing travel nurses, locums, and permanent clinical roles
