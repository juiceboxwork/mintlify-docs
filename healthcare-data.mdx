Juicebox is piloting healthcare data for millions of US healthcare professionals. You can search by clinical credentials, review each provider's clinical background on their profile, and run outreach without leaving Juicebox.

This includes providers who may not appear in traditional searches, such as clinicians with no LinkedIn profile.

## What you can do

- **Search millions of US healthcare professionals**, including providers who never showed up in your searches before.
- **Filter on the clinical credentials that qualify them**, such as state license, specialty, board certification, training, and hospital privileges.
- **Reach out knowing their practice.** Every profile carries the clinical record, so your message lands on their specialty.

## How to search

Describe who you are looking for in plain language. For the best results, name the role, the state, and the credential that decides the hire. Start broad, review the pool, then narrow.

Example searches:

- "Physicians certified in family medicine, licensed in Texas, with experience in senior living or value-based primary care"
- "Dentists licensed in Arizona who are in private practice"
- "Nurse practitioners licensed in Florida who work in cardiology"

Juicebox turns your search into filters and ranking criteria automatically. You can review and adjust them any time.

## Filter on clinical credentials

Open the **Healthcare** filter group in Search to filter on:

| Filter | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Role** | RN, Nurse Practitioner, Physician, Behavioral Health |
| **Licensed in** | Texas, California, New York |
| **Formerly licensed in** | North Carolina, Tennessee |
| **Practicing in** | Los Angeles, Rochester, Minnesota |
| **Specialty & training** | Emergency Medicine, Oncology, Pediatric Neurosurgery, Cardiology Fellowship |
| **Clinical credentials** | Board certified, ACLS, BLS, CCRN |
| **Affiliated with** | Mayo Clinic, UCSF, Cleveland Clinic |
| **Requirements** | Hospital privileges, nursing compact license, private practice background, travel or contract background |

<Note>
  **Licensed in** and **Practicing in** are different filters. Licensed in is where a provider holds a state license to practice. Practicing in is where they actually work. A provider can be licensed in a state where they do not currently practice.
</Note>

### Match any or all license states

When you select more than one state in **Licensed in**, use the toggle next to the filter to choose how Juicebox matches them:

- **Any (OR)**: Matches providers licensed in at least one of the selected states. This is the default.
- **All (AND)**: Matches only providers with license evidence in every selected state.

Use **All (AND)** when a role requires coverage across several states, such as a telehealth nurse who must be licensed in Texas, Florida, and Georgia. When **All (AND)** is on, the search summary shows the filter as **Licensed in (all)**.

You can also ask for this in plain language, for example: "RNs licensed in both Texas and Florida."

### Find clinicians who were formerly licensed in a state

Use **Formerly licensed in** to find clinicians with past ties to a state. It matches providers who held a state license there that has since expired or lapsed, and who hold no active license in that state now. Clinicians who once practiced in an area are often more open to relocating back, which helps when you are filling roles in thin markets.

When you select more than one state, Juicebox matches providers formerly licensed in any of them (OR).

**Formerly licensed in** differs from **Licensed in**:

- **Licensed in** matches providers who currently hold a license, or an active nursing compact license, that covers the state.
- **Formerly licensed in** matches providers whose license in the state is no longer active. Juicebox excludes anyone who still holds an active license there. For NLC states, Juicebox also excludes nurses who are still authorized there through an active compact license.

You can also ask for this in plain language, for example: "RNs who used to be licensed in North Carolina."

### Filter on nursing compact licenses

Select **Has nursing compact license** under **Requirements** to find RNs and LPN/LVNs who hold an active, unencumbered Nurse Licensure Compact (NLC) multistate license. Juicebox excludes compact privileges that are expired, inactive, suspended, restricted, or otherwise encumbered.

Use this filter when you need nurses who can practice across participating NLC states without a separate license in each one. You can also ask for it in plain language, for example: "Travel RNs with a multistate compact license."

When you filter by **Licensed in**, Juicebox also counts an active nursing compact license as an alternate way to match any selected state that participates in the NLC. For example, a search for RNs licensed in Texas also returns RNs who hold a compact license from another NLC state.

### Pick a role or role group

The **Role** filter includes individual professions, such as RN or Physician, and umbrella role groups that cover several related professions in one pick:

- **Behavioral Health**
- **Nurses (RN / LPN)**
- **Therapists (PT / OT / SLP / RT)**
- **Allied Health**
- **Other Clinicians**
- **Advanced Practice Nurses (CRNA / Midwife / Clinical Nurse Specialist)**

When you select more than one role, Juicebox matches providers in any of them (OR). For example, pick **Behavioral Health** instead of selecting each behavioral health profession separately.

### Pick clinical credentials by family

Each option in **Clinical credentials** is a credential family. One pick covers every known spelling of that credential in the source records. For example, **Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN / LVN)** matches records listed as LPN, LVN, or LPN Multistate, and **Basic Life Support (BLS)** matches BLS, BLS Provider, and BLS for Healthcare Providers. You do not need to select each variation separately.

### Browse the Specialty and Role lists

Click into the **Specialty** or **Role** field without typing to browse the full list of options:

- **Specialty** lists specialties alphabetically, with subspecialties nested under their parent specialty.
- **Role** lists professions, with member professions nested under their role group.

Picking a parent covers everything beneath it. When you select a parent specialty or role group, Juicebox shows its children as checked, so you do not need to add them separately.

**Specialty** matches the specialty on a provider's license or board certification. If you type a specialty or role that is not in the list, the picker offers **Add "[your text]" as a criterion**. Click it to add the typed text as a ranking criterion in one step. See [Filters vs criteria](/filters-vs-criteria) for how the two differ.

### Contact filters for healthcare profiles

If your organization has Healthcare Data enabled, the **Work Email** and **Phone Number** contact filters also match contact data from healthcare registries:

- **Work Email** also matches emails listed in public registry records.
- **Phone Number** also matches registry phone numbers. These may be practice office lines rather than direct numbers.

**Personal Email** does not match registry contact data.

## Review the healthcare profile

Every healthcare professional's record includes a **Healthcare** tab with a birds eye view of their clinical background:

- Specialty
- Credentials and certifications
- Specialties and training
- Practice authorization, including license status and expiration. Nurses with an active NLC multistate license show a **Multi-state compact privilege** badge, which links to the [Nurse Licensure Compact website](https://www.nursecompact.com/).
- Practice relationships, such as hospital privileges

## Reach out from Juicebox

Once you have found the right providers, add them to a shortlist or a sequence directly from the search results. Because each profile carries the clinical record, you can tailor your outreach to their specialty without switching tools.

## Who this is for

- **Health systems and hospitals** hiring clinical staff against license and privilege requirements
- **Digital health, Pharma, Biotech, and Medical device companies** hiring clinicians, medical affairs, and regulatory roles
- **Healthcare staffing and recruiting firms** placing travel nurses, locums, and permanent clinical roles
