Juicebox supports data migration from most major recruitment CRMs.

As a Business plan customer, you have access to full assistance with data migration. If you're not on a Business plan, see [Migrating your Data to Juicebox (Starter & Growth)](/help/data-migration/migrate-starter-growth).

Reach out to your Juicebox representative to help guide you through this process. We'll schedule a call to help understand your data, structure, and options for migration.

<Note>
  For some migrations, an additional fee will apply.
</Note>

## Process overview

1. **Identify data for migration.** Typically, customers migrate two data types:
   - **Contacts**: Profiles you've interacted with previously.
   - **Activities**: Interactions such as emails, notes, and calls.
2. **Export data.** Usually available directly from your existing CRM. If direct export isn't possible, contact your current CRM provider.
3. **Import to Juicebox.** Provide your data to your Juicebox representative in **CSV or JSONL format**. If your data structure differs, inform us — we can accommodate special cases. Data import typically takes **2–3 business days**, depending on volume and complexity.
4. **Troubleshooting.** After import, we'll perform live checks to ensure accuracy and satisfaction.
5. **Process completed.** Your data migration is complete, and your Juicebox account is ready for use.

## Get started with data migration

To get started, reach out to your Juicebox representative or sales contact.

If you're not sure who that is, email [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai) and mention that you're interested in data migration.
