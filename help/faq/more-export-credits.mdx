If you need additional export credits, there are several options available depending on your current plan. For the number of export credits included in each plan, see [All About Credits](/all-about-credits).

## On the Growth plan

You can add more credits by purchasing additional seats — each additional seat includes its own monthly allotment of contact and export credits. See [How can I upgrade my plan?](/help/plans/upgrade-plan) for the steps.

<Note>
  Export credits cannot be purchased separately; adding seats is the way to increase your allotment on the Growth plan.
</Note>

## On the free plan

Free plan users will need to upgrade to a paid plan (Starter or Growth) to receive additional credits.

## For high-volume usage

Consider the [Business plan](/help/plans/business-plan), which includes custom export credit volumes. Contact our sales team at [sales@juicebox.work](mailto:sales@juicebox.work) for more information.
