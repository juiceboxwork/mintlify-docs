sidebarTitle: "Free trial limits"

---

The free trial of Juicebox is **usage-based with no time limit** — it allows you to explore the platform's core features until you reach the following usage limits:

- Up to 5 searches (including tweaking search filters and viewing additional pages of results)
- View up to 65 profiles
- 5 contact credits

Your trial will end when you reach **either** the search limit **or** the profile view limit **or** the contact credit limit, whichever comes first.

## What's included in the trial?

During your trial, you'll have access to key features including search functionality and AI filters. See [Getting Started](/getting-started) for a tour of the platform.

## What happens after the trial?

Once you reach any of the usage limits (searches, profile views, or contact credits), your trial will end immediately and you'll need to upgrade to a paid plan to continue using the platform. The trial is usage-based and ends when you reach any of these limits, not after a specific time period. Paid plans are available with both [monthly and annual billing](/help/billing/annual-billing) options.

<Note>
  Paid subscriptions auto-renew on your billing cycle date unless manually cancelled.
</Note>

## Can I try the product for just one month?

Yes. Our monthly subscription plan allows you to use the product for as little as one month:

1. Sign up for the monthly plan.
2. Use the product for as long as you need.
3. [Cancel through your account settings](/help/billing/cancel-subscription) before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next month.

Unless you cancel before the renewal date, your subscription renews monthly with a new charge. Cancellation doesn't immediately remove your access — you retain full features through the end of your current billing period.
