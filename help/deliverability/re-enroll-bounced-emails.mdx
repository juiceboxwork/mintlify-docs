---
title: "How can I re-enroll bounced emails?"
sidebarTitle: "Re-enroll bounced emails"
description: "Put profiles whose emails bounced back into a sequence."
---

You can re-enroll emails that have bounced into a sequence.

To do so, go to the sequence menu, select the profiles that have bounced, and click **Actions > Re-enroll in sequence**.

![Re-enroll in sequence action](/images/help/deliverability/re-enroll-bounced-emails-1.png)

Finally, select the desired sequence, preview, and send.

<Tip>
  Before re-enrolling, consider what caused the bounce in the first place — see [Why does an email bounce?](/help/deliverability/email-bounces) and [How can I minimize bounces?](/help/deliverability/minimize-bounces) You can also enable **Auto-enroll with fallback after email bounce** in your [organization settings](/manage-account#general).
</Tip>
