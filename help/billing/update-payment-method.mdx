---
title: "How can I update my payment method or billing info?"
sidebarTitle: "Update payment method"
description: "Change your payment method, edit billing information, or manage your subscription."
---

To manage your payment method, billing information, or subscription, click on your name/organization at the bottom left corner of the screen.

![Open the account menu](/images/help/billing/update-payment-method-1.png)

Next, select **Account Settings > Plan and Billing**. From here, you can:

- **Manage Billing** — change your billing information or payment method, or cancel your subscription.
- **Change Plan** — upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and annual billing.

![Plan and Billing settings](/images/help/billing/update-payment-method-2.png)

From the **Manage Billing** portal, you can update your card on file and billing details.

![Update billing information](/images/help/billing/update-payment-method-3.png)

<Note>
  Cancelling your subscription from this page prevents future charges while keeping your account active until the next renewal date. See [How can I cancel my subscription?](/help/billing/cancel-subscription) for details.
</Note>

If your issue is not resolved, reach out to us at [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai).
