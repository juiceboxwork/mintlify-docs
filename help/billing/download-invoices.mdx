---
title: "How can I access my invoice?"
sidebarTitle: "Download invoices"
description: "Find and download invoices for your Juicebox subscription."
---

To access your invoice:

1. Select your profile icon on the bottom left of the screen.

   ![Open the account menu](/images/help/billing/download-invoices-1.png)

2. Under **Plan and Billing**, select **View Plans** for the relevant invoice.

   ![View Plans under Plan and Billing](/images/help/billing/download-invoices-2.png)

3. Click **Download Invoice**.

   ![Download Invoice button](/images/help/billing/download-invoices-3.png)

In case you have any further questions, reach out to us at [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai).
