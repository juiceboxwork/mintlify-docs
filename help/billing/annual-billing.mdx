---
title: "How does annual billing work?"
sidebarTitle: "Annual billing"
description: "Understand the discount and upfront credits that come with annual plans."
---

When you subscribe to a plan on Juicebox, you have the option to subscribe on a monthly basis or annually.

Annual plans have two advantages:

1. **A discount of up to 25%** (depends on the chosen plan — see [current pricing](https://juicebox.ai/pricing)).
2. **All contact credits granted upfront.** Instead of receiving credits month by month, the full year's quantity of credits is immediately deposited in your account.

For example, if a plan costs \$199/month on monthly billing and \$179/month billed annually, subscribing annually saves you 20% of the total annual cost — and your entire 12-month credit allotment is available on day one.

To learn how credits work in detail, see [All About Credits](/all-about-credits). Ready to make the switch? See [How do I switch between monthly and annual billing?](/help/billing/switch-billing-frequency)
