title: "How can I cancel my subscription?"
sidebarTitle: "Cancel subscription"

---

To cancel your subscription, select your name/organization at the bottom left corner of the screen.

![Open the account menu](/images/help/billing/cancel-subscription-1.png)

Next, select **Account Settings**, then **Plan and Billing**, then **Manage Billing**.

![Plan and Billing settings](/images/help/billing/cancel-subscription-2.png)

From here, you can change your billing information, payment method, or cancel your plan.

![Manage Billing options](/images/help/billing/cancel-subscription-3.png)

You now have the option to either cancel your plan or pause it.

<Warning>
  Pausing your subscription is different from cancelling it. If you pause your subscription, you will still be charged for renewal after the pause period ends. To fully cancel and avoid future charges, you must explicitly cancel the subscription by selecting **Decline Offer** — not just pause it.
</Warning>

Select **Decline Offer** if you wish to cancel.

![Decline Offer to complete cancellation](/images/help/billing/cancel-subscription-4.png)

**What to expect:** When you cancel your subscription, you will not be charged again and your cancellation will take effect at your next renewal date. You will continue to have access to your subscription until that renewal date. Note that cancelling prevents future charges but does not provide a refund for the current billing period.

**What happens to my data?** All your account data, including projects and sequences, remains accessible after cancellation. If you reactivate your subscription in the future, you'll regain full access to all your previous data.

If you have any further questions, reach out to us at [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai). Our support team can also assist with cancelling your subscription and processing refunds for recent charges if needed.
