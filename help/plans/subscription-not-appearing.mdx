---
title: "Why is my premium subscription not appearing?"
sidebarTitle: "Subscription not appearing"
description: "Confirm your subscription is active, and what to do if it isn't."
---

To confirm that your subscription has been activated, click on your profile icon on the bottom left of the screen.

![Open the account menu](/images/help/plans/subscription-not-appearing-1.png)

Then, under **Your Account**, you will see your active subscription.

![Active subscription under Your Account](/images/help/plans/subscription-not-appearing-2.png)

If you have subscribed to a premium plan but are not seeing it activate, contact us via the in-app **Contact Support** button, or by reaching out to [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai).

<Tip>
  If a teammate purchased the plan, make sure you've accepted their workspace invitation — check your [pending invites](https://app.juicebox.ai/account?tab=invites). See [Add team members](/help/plans/add-team-members) for details.
</Tip>
