## Agents on the Business plan

The Business plan is sales-led and supports higher-volume Agent configurations. Reach out to [sales@juicebox.work](mailto:sales@juicebox.work) to discuss custom Agent capacity.

The 50 and 75 leads-per-day cadences are available on any plan, including Starter and Growth, once your organization has purchased enough Agent seats.

Starter and Growth customers can preview Agents with a monthly free-leads trial (cadences of 5, 15, or 25). See [Trying Agents without an Agent seat](/juicebox-agents#trying-agents-without-an-agent-seat) for what's included.
