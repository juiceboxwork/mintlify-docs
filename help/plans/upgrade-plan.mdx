## Free plan

The Free plan gives your organization **4 searches per calendar month**. The allowance is org-wide and shared across everyone in the org.

### Monthly reset

Free-plan orgs get a fresh allowance at the start of every UTC calendar month. Juicebox applies the reset lazily on the first request your org makes on or after the 1st. If your org has already used all 4 searches, you pick the next allowance up the first time anyone visits Juicebox after the month turns over.

### How searches are counted

Each results view spends the org's allowance one page at a time:

- Running a new search consumes one search from the allowance.
- Each page turn within a results view consumes one more search. Paid plans are unaffected: pagination does not consume anything on a paid plan.
- Pages you've already unlocked stay free to revisit inside the same results view.
- If you run out mid-search, the current results stay visible and the upgrade prompt opens over them. You don't lose the search you're looking at.

### Prompts to upgrade

Free-plan users see two distinct upgrade surfaces:

- An **Upgrade** button in the sidebar footer, available on every page.
- A paywall snackbar on the search results view, which appears once the org's allowance is spent.

### Re-activation walkthrough

Returning free-plan users see a one-time re-activation modal that walks through what's new in six steps: [Agents](/juicebox-agents) (Agent 4.0), the reset free searches, [hiring manager workflow](/hiring-managers), [Research Signals](/research-signals), [Talent Flow](/insights), and [Network Sourcing](/network-sourcing). The first step introduces Agent 4.0 with a **Try now** button that opens a new agent, alongside a **Learn more** link to the launch post. The other steps have a **Learn more** link into these docs.

Dismissing the modal is deliberate. Use the close (×) button or one of its call-to-action buttons. Backdrop clicks and the Escape key do not close it. After the first time you complete or dismiss it, subsequent monthly resets show a short toast (`Your 4 free searches have been reset`) instead of the full walkthrough.

### Agent 4.0 announcement

Users who are outside the re-activation audience see the Agent 4.0 launch as a separate in-app takeover on the home screen. It shows the launch banner with a **Try now** button that opens an agent in your most recent project (or creates one if you have none open) and a **Learn more** link to the launch post. The takeover shows once per user and waits for any first-time onboarding to finish before it opens.

## Upgrade or downgrade an existing premium plan

If you are already subscribed to a premium plan, select your profile icon in the bottom left corner:

![Open the account menu](/images/help/plans/upgrade-plan-2.png)

From there, select **Account Settings**, then **Plan and Billing**, and finally **Modify Plan**. You can also navigate directly to your [billing settings](https://app.juicebox.ai/account?tab=billing).

![Modify Plan in billing settings](/images/help/plans/upgrade-plan-3.png)

<Note>
  This same process can be used to upgrade to a higher-tier plan or downgrade to a lower-tier plan.
</Note>

**Billing and proration:** When you upgrade to a higher-tier plan mid-cycle (for example, from Starter to Growth), you'll be charged a prorated amount — the difference between what you've already paid for your current plan and the cost of the new plan for the remaining time in your billing period. Your next renewal will charge the full amount for the new subscription. Credits are also prorated mid-cycle — you'll receive a prorated credit allocation for the remainder of your billing period, and the full credit amount at renewal.

## Managing credits through plan changes

### Adding credits on the Growth plan

To add more credits to your account, you can add an additional seat on the Growth plan. Each additional seat includes its own monthly allotment of contact and export credits — see [All About Credits](/all-about-credits) for current amounts per plan.

The charge for the upgrade and the credits you receive are both prorated based on the remainder of your current billing cycle. Once your renewal hits, you'll be charged in full for all seats on the plan and receive the full credit amount for each seat.

### Unlimited credits

For unlimited credit options, the [Business plan](/help/plans/business-plan) is available.
