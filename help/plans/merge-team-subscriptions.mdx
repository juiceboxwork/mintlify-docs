---
title: "My team member already has a subscription. How can I add them to my team plan?"
sidebarTitle: "Merge team subscriptions"
description: "Combine separate paid subscriptions into one team workspace."
---

If your team member already has a paid subscription on Juicebox, contact our support team at [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai) to merge your organizations.

Alternatively, you can ask them to [cancel their current subscription](/help/billing/cancel-subscription). Then, send them an [invite](/manage-account#team-members) to join your organization. They will need to accept the invite in the settings page.

For the full invitation flow, see [How can I add team members to the Growth Plan?](/help/plans/add-team-members)
