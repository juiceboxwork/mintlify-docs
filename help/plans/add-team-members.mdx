The Growth plan allows for **up to five (5) seats**, and each seat comes with an additional set of contact and export credits (see [All About Credits](/all-about-credits) for current amounts). If you would like to add more than five team members, consider upgrading to the [Business plan](/help/plans/business-plan).

<Note>
  The Starter plan only allows for one user. To add team members, you must first [upgrade](/help/plans/upgrade-plan) to the Growth plan.
</Note>

## Inviting team members

If your team member already has an account on Juicebox (including a free account), you can invite them directly through the **Team Members** settings. When they accept your invite, they will be automatically moved from their current plan to your Growth plan workspace. The same invitation process works for both new users and existing account holders.

If your team member does not have an account on Juicebox yet, click on your name/organization at the bottom left corner.

![Open the account menu](/images/help/plans/add-team-members-1.png)

Next, select **Invite Members**. You can then send an email invitation to your team member to join the organization with you.

When inviting a team member, a popup will appear indicating you'll be charged based on your contract. Click accept to complete the invitation.

## Adding additional seats

If you need to purchase additional seats before inviting team members, you can do so through your account settings without contacting support:

1. Click on your profile icon at the lower left corner of Juicebox, then select **Account Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Plan and Billing**.
3. Select **Modify Plan**.
4. Increase the number of seats to your desired amount.
5. Confirm the changes.

## After sending invitations

Once you send the invitation, the team member will need to accept it to join your organization. If they log in and see a "free trial" status instead of being part of your Growth plan organization, they should check for [pending invites](https://app.juicebox.ai/account?tab=invites) and accept the invitation.

![Pending invitations](/images/help/plans/add-team-members-2.png)

### Resend a pending invitation

If a teammate hasn't accepted yet, you can send them a reminder. Any member of your organization can resend an invitation:

1. Go to **Account Settings** > **Team Members** and open the **Pending invitations** tab.
2. Find the invitation and click the **Resend invite** (send) icon on its row.

The teammate receives the same invitation email with "Reminder:" in the subject line. The **Status** column shows when the invitation was sent and when it was last reminded, for example "Invited 2 months ago · Reminded just now".

<Note>
  You can remind each invitation once every 24 hours. During the cooldown, the **Resend invite** icon is disabled and shows "Resent in the last 24 hours".
</Note>

### Remind all invitations older than 30 days

Admins and view-only admins see a banner at the top of the **Pending invitations** table when any invitations have been pending for more than 30 days. The banner shows how many invitations are older than 30 days and how many can be reminded now.

1. Click **Send N reminders** in the banner, where N is the number of invitations that can be reminded.
2. Confirm in the dialog.

Juicebox resends every invitation older than 30 days that isn't in its 24-hour cooldown. This includes all pending invitations, not only those matching your current search. Each click sends up to 100 reminders. If more remain, click the button again to remind the rest. If every old invitation was reminded in the last 24 hours, the button is disabled.

<Tip>
  Does your team member already have their own paid subscription? See [how to merge team subscriptions](/help/plans/merge-team-subscriptions).
</Tip>
