Agents are an optional add-on to your paid Juicebox subscription. Each Agent seat lets one Agent source, pause, or await action at a time. Learn more in [Juicebox Agents](/juicebox-agents).

## Business plan

How you add Agents on the Business plan depends on your role and whether your plan already includes Agents.

### Admins with at least one Agent

If you're an org admin and your plan already includes at least one Agent, you can increase your Agent count directly in-app. You don't need a sales call:

1. Click on your profile icon at the lower left corner of Juicebox, then select **Account Settings**.
2. Navigate to the **Agents** tab and select **Manage agents**. You can also start from the **Add more Agents to your plan** dialog that appears when you hit your Agent limit.
3. Use the stepper to choose how many Agents to add.
4. Review the new total, then check **I acknowledge the additional per-Agent costs in my contract**.
5. Select **Confirm Agents**.

Your Agent limit increases immediately, and Juicebox sends you a confirmation email. Juicebox then issues an invoice for the added Agents, prorated for your current billing period. The invoice finalizes automatically about 24 hours after your confirmation.

### Admins with no Agents

If your Business plan doesn't include any Agents yet, the dialog prompts you to **Request a trial** instead. This opens a booking page to schedule a call with the Juicebox team, who will set up Agents for your organization.

### Members

If you're not an org admin, the dialog asks you to choose one of your organization's admins. Juicebox emails that admin your request, and they can complete the increase themselves.

## Growth and Starter plans

On the Growth and Starter plans, the Agent purchase flow is unchanged. Open the purchase dialog, choose the number of Agents, and complete the checkout.
