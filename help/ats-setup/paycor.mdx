## About the integration

Connect Paycor to Juicebox to import job descriptions into your searches and identify profiles that already exist in your ATS.

You can also export profiles to a selected Paycor job.

If Paycor is not visible in your Juicebox Integrations tab, contact [Juicebox Support](mailto:support@juicebox.ai) to confirm availability for your account.

## Before you start

You need a **Paycor Perform HR, Payroll, or Company administrator** account to authorize the connection.

Open **Integrations** in Juicebox, select **Paycor**, and click **Activate**. Keep the connection pop-up open throughout setup.

<Note>
Some Paycor connections go directly to **Sign in with Paycor**. Complete the developer-portal steps below only if your connection pop-up requests them.
</Note>

## Create a Developer Portal account, if requested

1. Open [Paycor Developer Portal](https://developers.paycor.com/). For a sandbox connection, use [the sandbox portal](https://developers-sandbox.paycor.com/).
2. If you do not already have a portal account, choose **Sign Up** for existing Paycor clients, then **Get Started**.
3. Sign in with your Paycor account, select the portal administrator, and click **Request Access**. Paycor sends an email to that administrator.
4. Once you have access, return to the connection pop-up and continue.

## Create an application, if requested

1. In the Developer Portal, open **Applications** and click **+ Application**.
2. Give the application a recognizable name, such as **Juicebox**. Select **Standard Application**, then create it.
3. Copy the application OAuth **Client ID** and **Client Secret** into the corresponding fields in the Juicebox connection pop-up.

## Configure the return URL and subscription key, if requested

1. Open the application's **Security Connections** tab.
2. Under **Approved Return OAuth URL**, add the personalized redirect URL shown in your connection pop-up. Save the change.
3. Copy the **APIm Subscription Key** from Security Connections into the connection pop-up and continue.

## Configure data access, if requested

1. Open the application's **Data Access** tab.
2. Enable the access scopes listed in your connection pop-up and save. The required list depends on your connection.
3. On the **General** tab, copy the **Scope Name** into the connection pop-up and continue.

## Authorize Paycor

1. In the connection pop-up, click **Sign in with Paycor**.
2. Sign in and authorize access to the legal entities and ATS accounts you intend to connect.
3. Finish the authorization window and return to Juicebox. Confirm that the integration shows **Connected**.

## Confirm the integration

After the initial sync, check that the expected jobs and existing candidates appear. Export a test profile to a selected Paycor job and confirm that it is associated with the intended job.

If authorization fails, check the return URL, selected entities, and requested scopes against your current connection pop-up.

## Using the integration

To start a search from a job, open **Projects** and select **Import Job from ATS / CRM**, then choose the relevant Paycor job.

Juicebox identifies profiles that already exist in Paycor, helping your team check for existing records before reaching out.

To send a profile to Paycor, follow the [export steps](/exports) and choose the Paycor export option for the linked job. Confirm that the profile appears in Paycor after the export completes.

Resume attachments and exports without a job are not currently supported.

<Note>
The initial sync can take time. Data may continue to appear after the connection is established. If you do not see the expected records, contact Juicebox Support.
</Note>
