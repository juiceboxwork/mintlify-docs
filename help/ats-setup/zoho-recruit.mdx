## About the integration

The Zoho Recruit and Juicebox integration is designed to seamlessly connect both systems in a two-way sync. You can use the integration for three use cases:

1. **Import Project from ATS**: Import a job description from Zoho Recruit into Juicebox to automatically kick off a search using the job description. This also includes linking your Juicebox project to an ATS job, which makes exporting profiles to your ATS easier. More details [here](/projects).
2. **Export Profiles to ATS**: Save profiles directly into Zoho Recruit, including contact details, current company and job, profile URLs, and more. On some plans, this can be customized further based on your preferred fields. Juicebox also has the ability to auto-export to your ATS, including exporting all shortlisted profiles or exporting only interested profiles.
3. **Import Candidates**: View profiles already in Zoho Recruit within Juicebox. Avoid reaching out to the same profile twice, and instantly view your relationship history.

### Start a project via the Zoho Recruit integration

Open the projects page and select **Import Job from ATS / CRM**. You will then see your Zoho Recruit jobs and can select the corresponding option.

### Export profiles to Zoho Recruit

Follow the regular [export](/exports) steps and select **Export to Zoho Recruit** on either an individual profile or a bulk export. After exporting, you will see the profile created in your Zoho Recruit account under **Candidates**.

### View profiles already in Zoho Recruit

Juicebox will tag profiles that already exist in Zoho Recruit. You will see the last modified date, as well as the option to view the profile directly in Zoho Recruit by clicking the **View in ATS** button.

## Setting up the integration

1. Navigate to the **Integrations** tab on the Juicebox platform.
2. Select **Zoho Recruit** and click **Activate**. You can also use the search bar at the top.
3. You will see a pop-up asking to activate your integration. Note that you must be an administrator of Zoho Recruit in order to link to Juicebox successfully.
4. After reviewing privacy information, follow the steps in the pop-up to sign in to Zoho Recruit and authorize the connection.
5. After you have clicked through the remaining pages of the pop-up, the configuration will be completed. If successful, you will see a green **Connected** button in your Integrations marketplace.

Congratulations! You have successfully integrated Zoho Recruit and Juicebox.

<Note>
  It may take a few minutes for all data to sync between Juicebox and Zoho Recruit.
</Note>
