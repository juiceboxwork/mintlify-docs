## About the integration

The JobAdder and Juicebox integration is designed to seamlessly connect both systems in a two-way sync. You can use the integration for three use cases:

1. **Import Project from ATS**: Import a job description from JobAdder into Juicebox to automatically kick off a search using the job description. This also includes linking your Juicebox project to an ATS job, which makes exporting profiles to your ATS easier. More details [here](/projects).
2. **Export Profiles to ATS**: Save profiles directly into JobAdder, including contact details, current company and job, profile URLs, and more. On some plans, this can be customized further based on your preferred fields. Juicebox also has the ability to auto-export to your ATS, including exporting all shortlisted profiles or exporting only interested profiles.
3. **Import Candidates**: View profiles already in JobAdder within Juicebox. Avoid reaching out to the same profile twice, and instantly view your relationship history.

### Start a project via the JobAdder integration

Open the projects page and select **Import Job from ATS / CRM**. You will then see your JobAdder jobs and can select the corresponding option.

### Export profiles to JobAdder

Follow the regular [export](/exports) steps and select **Export to JobAdder** on either an individual profile or a bulk export. After exporting, you will see the profile created in your JobAdder account.

### View profiles already in JobAdder

Juicebox will tag profiles that already exist in JobAdder. You will see the last modified date, as well as the option to view the profile directly in JobAdder by clicking the **View in ATS** button.

## Before you start

Make sure you can sign in to your JobAdder account. Use a JobAdder account that has access to the data and actions you want to use.

## Setting up the integration

1. Navigate to the **Integrations** tab on the Juicebox platform.
2. Select **JobAdder** and click **Activate**. You can also use the search bar at the top.

   <Frame>
     ![JobAdder connection pop-up](/images/help/ats-setup/jobadder-1.png)
   </Frame>

3. Click **Sign in with JobAdder**. If JobAdder asks you to sign in, enter your JobAdder login details.

   <Frame>
     ![Sign in with JobAdder button](/images/help/ats-setup/jobadder-2.png)
   </Frame>

4. Review the requested permissions and click **Accept**. The permissions shown can differ depending on the data and actions required for your use case.

   <Frame>
     ![Reviewing and accepting the requested permissions](/images/help/ats-setup/jobadder-3.png)
   </Frame>

5. Once you accept the permissions, the JobAdder window closes and the connection is established. If successful, you will see a green **Connected** button in your Integrations marketplace.

Congratulations! You have successfully integrated JobAdder and Juicebox.

<Note>
  It may take a few minutes for all data to sync between Juicebox and JobAdder.
</Note>
