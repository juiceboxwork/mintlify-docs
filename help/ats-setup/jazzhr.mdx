## About the integration

The JazzHR and Juicebox integration is designed to seamlessly connect both systems in a two-way sync. You can use the integration for three use cases:

1. **Import Project from ATS**: Import a job description from JazzHR into Juicebox to automatically kick off a search using the job description. This also includes linking your Juicebox project to an ATS job, which makes exporting profiles to your ATS easier. More details [here](/projects).
2. **Export Profiles to ATS**: Save profiles directly into JazzHR, including contact details, current company and job, profile URLs, and more. On some plans, this can be customized further based on your preferred fields. Juicebox also has the ability to auto-export to your ATS, including exporting all shortlisted profiles or exporting only interested profiles.
3. **Import Candidates**: View profiles already in JazzHR within Juicebox. Avoid reaching out to the same profile twice, and instantly view your relationship history.

### Start a project via the JazzHR integration

Open the projects page and select **Import Job from ATS / CRM**. You will then see your JazzHR jobs and can select the corresponding option.

### Export profiles to JazzHR

Follow the regular [export](/exports) steps and select **Export to JazzHR** on either an individual profile or a bulk export. After exporting, you will see the profile created in your JazzHR account.

### View profiles already in JazzHR

Juicebox will tag profiles that already exist in JazzHR. You will see the last modified date, as well as the option to view the profile directly in JazzHR by clicking the **View in ATS** button.

## Before you start

Your account must have at least the **Recruiting Administrator** role in your company's JazzHR instance. To check:

1. Log in to JazzHR and click **SETTINGS** at the top of the screen.

   <Frame>
     ![SETTINGS link at the top of JazzHR](/images/help/ats-setup/jazzhr-1.png)
   </Frame>

2. Check that you can see the **INTEGRATIONS** link on the Settings page.

   <Frame>
     ![INTEGRATIONS link on the Settings page](/images/help/ats-setup/jazzhr-2.png)
   </Frame>

If you cannot see the **SETTINGS** link on the home page, or the **INTEGRATIONS** link on the Settings page, your account does not have enough permissions. Find out who has the **Recruiting Administrator** or **Super Administrator** role in your company's JazzHR instance, and ask them to promote you or take over the setup process.

## Find your API key

1. Log in to JazzHR and click **SETTINGS** at the top of the screen.

   <Frame>
     ![SETTINGS link at the top of JazzHR](/images/help/ats-setup/jazzhr-1.png)
   </Frame>

2. Click the **INTEGRATIONS** link on the Settings page.

   <Frame>
     ![INTEGRATIONS link on the Settings page](/images/help/ats-setup/jazzhr-2.png)

   <Frame>

   <Frame>

   </Frame>

## Setting up the integration

1. Navigate to the **Integrations** tab on the Juicebox platform.
2. Select **JazzHR** and click **Activate**. You can also use the search bar at the top.
3. Follow the instructions in the connection pop-up, entering your API key and career page URL, then click **Set up integration**.

   <Frame>
     ![Set up integration button in the connection flow](/images/help/ats-setup/jazzhr-9.png)
   </Frame>

   <Frame>
     ![Completed JazzHR connection](/images/help/ats-setup/jazzhr-10.png)
   </Frame>

4. After you have clicked through the remaining pages of the pop-up, the configuration will be completed. If successful, you will see a green **Connected** button in your Integrations marketplace.

Congratulations! You have successfully integrated JazzHR and Juicebox.

<Note>
  It may take a few minutes for all data to sync between Juicebox and JazzHR.
</Note>
