## About the integration

By integrating Jobvite with Juicebox, you can simplify your outbound sourcing strategy, improve quality of hire, and decrease time to hire. You can use the integration for three use cases:

1. **Import Project from ATS**: Import a job description from Jobvite into Juicebox to automatically kick off a search using the job description. This also includes linking your Juicebox project to an ATS job, which makes exporting profiles to your ATS easier. More details [here](/projects).
2. **Export Profiles to ATS**: Save profiles directly into Jobvite, including contact details, current company and job, profile URLs, and more. On some plans, this can be customized further based on your preferred fields. Juicebox also has the ability to auto-export to your ATS, including exporting all shortlisted profiles or exporting only interested profiles.
3. **Import Candidates**: View profiles already in Jobvite within Juicebox. Avoid reaching out to the same profile twice, and instantly view your relationship history.

### Start a project via the Jobvite integration

Navigate to the Projects page on Juicebox. On the right side, you'll find your synced jobs from Jobvite. Simply click on a job to create a new project — this will automatically initiate a search using the job description.

### Export profiles to Jobvite

Follow the regular [export](/exports) steps and select **Export to Jobvite** on either an individual profile or a bulk export. After exporting, you will see the profile created in your Jobvite account under **Contacts**.

<Note>
  You must be a Jobvite Engage user in order to access the Contacts tab.
</Note>

### View profiles already in Jobvite

Juicebox will tag profiles that already exist in Jobvite. You will see the last modified date, as well as the option to view the profile directly in Jobvite by clicking the **View in ATS** button.

<Note>
  Juicebox can only search through candidates saved in the Jobvite **Candidates** tab, not **Contacts**.
</Note>

## Setting up the integration

1. Navigate to the **Integrations** tab on the Juicebox platform.
2. Select **Jobvite** and click **Activate**. You can also use the search bar at the top.
3. You will see a pop-up asking to activate your integration. Note that you must be an administrator of Jobvite in order to link to Juicebox successfully.
4. After reviewing privacy information, you will be prompted to enter your API key and your secret key. To obtain these keys, contact Jobvite support directly.
5. After entering your keys, the integration will be completed.

Congratulations! You have successfully integrated Jobvite and Juicebox.

<Note>
  It may take a few minutes for all data to sync between Juicebox and Jobvite. Wait up to 10 minutes to ensure all data has been synced.
</Note>
