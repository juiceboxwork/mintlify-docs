## About the integration

The Dayforce and Juicebox integration is designed to seamlessly connect both systems in a two-way sync. You can use the integration for three use cases:

1. **Import Project from ATS**: Import a job description from Dayforce into Juicebox to automatically kick off a search using the job description. This also includes linking your Juicebox project to an ATS job, which makes exporting profiles to your ATS easier. More details [here](/projects).
2. **Export Profiles to ATS**: Save profiles directly into Dayforce, including contact details, current company and job, profile URLs, and more. On some plans, this can be customized further based on your preferred fields. Juicebox also has the ability to auto-export to your ATS, including exporting all shortlisted profiles or exporting only interested profiles.
3. **Import Candidates**: View profiles already in Dayforce within Juicebox. Avoid reaching out to the same profile twice, and instantly view your relationship history.

### Start a project via the Dayforce integration

Open the projects page and select **Import Job from ATS / CRM**. You will then see your Dayforce jobs and can select the corresponding option.

### Export profiles to Dayforce

Follow the regular [export](/exports) steps and select **Export to Dayforce** on either an individual profile or a bulk export. After exporting, you will see the profile created in your Dayforce account.

### View profiles already in Dayforce

Juicebox will tag profiles that already exist in Dayforce. You will see the last modified date, as well as the option to view the profile directly in Dayforce by clicking the **View in ATS** button.

## Before you start

Confirm you have Dayforce **Administrator** permissions before beginning the integration.

## Step 1: Create a new user

Go to **System Admin** > **User** and select **New**. This username and password will be used in the connection pop-up.

## Step 2: Configure a role

1. Open the menu on the left of your screen and click through to **System Admin** > **Roles**.

   <Frame>
     ![Roles under System Admin](/images/help/ats-setup/dayforce-1.png)
   </Frame>

2. Create a new role and navigate to the **Features** tab. Check the following:
   - **HCM Anywhere** > **Web Services** > **Patch/Post Candidate Sourcing and Job Application Sourcing**
   - **Recruiting**

   <Frame>
     ![Features tab with the required permissions checked](/images/help/ats-setup/dayforce-2.png)
   </Frame>

## Step 3: Adjust recruiting properties

Navigate to **System Admin** > **Roles** > **Recruiting Setup** > **Client Properties** and enable the following property:

- **Allow Candidates and Job Application Sourcing from External Job Boards**

## Step 4: Assign the new role to the new user

Navigate to the user created in Step 1 and assign the role you created in Steps 2 and 3.

## Step 5: Allow document types for upload

Navigate to **System Admin** > **Roles** > **Documents** > **Admin** > **Document Types**.

Open the "Resume" document type and add your role to **Roles** in the bottom right panel:

<Frame>
  ![Adding the role to the Resume document type](/images/help/ats-setup/dayforce-3.png)
</Frame>

## Setting up the integration

1. Navigate to the **Integrations** tab on the Juicebox platform.
2. Select **Dayforce** and click **Activate**. You can also use the search bar at the top.
3. In the connection pop-up, enter the username and password from Step 1. You also need to specify your **Client Name Space** and **Environment**.

   <Frame>
     ![Entering the credentials, Client Name Space, and Environment in the connection flow](/images/help/ats-setup/dayforce-4.png)
   </Frame>

4. After you have clicked through the remaining pages of the pop-up, the configuration will be completed. If successful, you will see a green **Connected** button in your Integrations marketplace.

Congratulations! You have successfully integrated Dayforce and Juicebox.

<Note>
  It may take a few minutes for all data to sync between Juicebox and Dayforce.
</Note>
