## About the integration

The supported workflows are confirmed during assisted onboarding for your PageUp environment.

## Before you start

PageUp is in **Closed Beta** and requires assisted onboarding. Contact Juicebox Support before requesting credentials or promising a particular workflow.

## Request PageUp onboarding

Contact [Juicebox Support](mailto:support@juicebox.ai) with your company name, PageUp environment, and PageUp administrator’s contact details. Explain whether you need job import, candidate export, resume export, or rediscovery.

## Confirm access and follow the supplied setup

Work with Juicebox and PageUp to confirm the supported scope for your environment. Once access is approved, use the account-specific connection instructions supplied by Juicebox and enter credentials only in the secure connection flow.

## Validate the agreed workflows

Complete a test of each workflow agreed with your support contact before rolling out the integration. Public Kombo documentation does not currently establish PageUp’s ATS model or candidate-action coverage. PageUp assessment instructions are for a different integration and should not be substituted.

## Support

For help, contact [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai). For an overview of available workflows, see [ATS Integrations](/ats-integrations).

Connector reference: [Kombo’s PageUp coverage documentation](https://docs.kombo.dev/ats/connectors/pageup).
