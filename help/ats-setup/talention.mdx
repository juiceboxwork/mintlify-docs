## About the integration

Connect Talention to Juicebox to import jobs and start searches from their job descriptions. You can also export profiles with resumes to a selected job. Existing candidates can be identified in Juicebox and used for rediscovery, subject to the access granted to the connection.

## Before you start

Arrange setup with your Talention administrator and Juicebox Support. Public connector documentation confirms integration capabilities but does not provide a verified account-connection walkthrough.

## Request the current connection instructions

Contact [Juicebox Support](mailto:support@juicebox.ai) with your company name, Talention instance, and administrator contact. Ask the team to confirm the authentication details and permissions required for your account.

## Complete the supplied connection flow

Open the account-specific connection link supplied by Juicebox, enter the credentials issued for this integration, and complete its checks. Use the instructions in that flow if your Talention menus differ; do not borrow setup steps from Talent Clue or TalentSoft.

## Validate access

Check the expected jobs, a known existing candidate, and an export to a selected job. Include a resume in the test if resume export is part of your workflow.

## Using the integration

Start from an imported job on the [Projects](/projects) page to create a search linked to that job.

To send a profile to Talention, follow the [export steps](/exports) and select the connected ATS and intended job.

Learn how to work with existing candidates in [Talent Rediscovery](/talent-rediscovery).
