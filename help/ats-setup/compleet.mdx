## About the integration

Connect Compleet to Juicebox to import jobs and start searches from their job descriptions. You can also export profiles with resumes to a selected job. This connector does not provide full-database candidate reading for rediscovery.

## Before you start

This guide applies to **Compleet Pitcher API**. Contact Juicebox Support before starting; this connector requires setup assistance.

## Request Pitcher setup

Tell [Juicebox Support](mailto:support@juicebox.ai) that you want to connect **Compleet using the Pitcher API**. Include your company name, Compleet product, and your administrator’s contact details. Ask the team to coordinate the job-feed and application-submission configuration with Compleet.

## Complete your account-specific connection

Once the configuration is ready, open the connection link supplied by Juicebox and enter the values issued for your Pitcher connection. Follow that window’s instructions and complete the connection with your support contact. The public PersoProfiler instructions apply to a different connector and should not be used for this setup.

## Validate jobs and exports with Support

Confirm that the expected jobs appear. Link a test project to a job, export a profile with a resume, and verify the resulting application in Compleet. Pitcher does not provide the candidate-reading coverage needed to search the full existing candidate database.

## Using the integration

Start from an imported job on the [Projects](/projects) page to create a search linked to that job.

To send a profile to Compleet, follow the [export steps](/exports) and select the connected ATS and intended job.
