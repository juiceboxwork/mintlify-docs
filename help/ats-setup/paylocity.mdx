## About the integration

This connection imports jobs. Candidate export, resume export, and rediscovery are not available through the documented ATS connector.

## Before you start

This ATS connection imports jobs. Ask your [Paylocity representative](mailto:service@paylocity.com) for your organization’s **job feed URL**.

Open **Integrations** in Juicebox, select **Paylocity**, and choose **Activate**. Keep the connection window open while completing the steps below. If the integration is not visible, ask [Juicebox Support](mailto:support@juicebox.ai) to confirm access for your account.

<Note>
The screenshots show vendor settings or the connection window provided by Kombo. Use the credentials, source labels, permissions, and URLs from your own connection; screenshot values are examples.
</Note>

## Connect the job feed

Once Paylocity provides the feed URL, paste the full URL into the Juicebox connection window and complete setup.

## Verify the imported jobs

Confirm that the jobs shown in Juicebox match the feed. This is separate from a Paylocity HRIS connection; candidate export and full-database rediscovery are not available through the documented ATS connector.

## Confirm the integration

Finish the connection window and return to Juicebox. Confirm that the connection has completed, then allow the initial sync to populate your data.

- Check that the expected jobs appear in Juicebox.

If validation fails, check the credentials, account or region, and permission list shown in the connection window. Contact Juicebox Support if the expected data is still missing.

## Using the integration

Start from an imported job on the [Projects](/projects) page to create a search linked to that job.
