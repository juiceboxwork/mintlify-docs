---
title: "How can I delete my account?"
sidebarTitle: "Delete your account"
description: "Permanently delete your Juicebox account and its data."
---

<Warning>
  Deleting your account will permanently delete all searches, shortlisted contacts, and profile data.
</Warning>

To delete your account, reach out to us using the in-app support button or by emailing [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai).

![Contact support to delete your account](/images/help/account/delete-account-1.png)

If you only want to stop being charged, you can [cancel your subscription](/help/billing/cancel-subscription) instead — your data stays intact in case you return later.
