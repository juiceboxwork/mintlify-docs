---
title: "How can I change my password?"
sidebarTitle: "Change your password"
description: "Reset your Juicebox password from your account settings."
---

<Note>
  You can only change your password if you signed up with the "sign up with email" option. If you signed up using Google, you'll need to change your password through Google.
</Note>

If you signed up with your email, follow these steps to change your password:

1. Select your profile in the bottom left of the screen.

   ![Open your profile menu in the bottom left corner](/images/help/account/change-password-1.png)

2. Select **Account Settings > Account > Password > Reset Password**.

   ![Reset Password in Account Settings](/images/help/account/change-password-2.png)

3. Check your inbox — a reset link is sent to your login email address.

If you're locked out of your account entirely, see [Troubleshooting login issues](/help/account/login-issues).

Still running into issues? Reach out via the in-app support button or email [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai).
