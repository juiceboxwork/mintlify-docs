---
title: "How can I change the email address associated with my account?"
sidebarTitle: "Change your email address"
description: "Switch your Juicebox login to a new email address."
---

To update the email address associated with your account:

1. Create a free account using the new email address you'd like to use for login.
2. Contact our support team by emailing [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai), or by using the **Contact Support** button in-app.

Once we receive your request, we can quickly make the switch for you.
