---
title: "How can I change my organization name?"
sidebarTitle: "Change organization name"
description: "Update your organization's name from your account settings."
---

To change your organization name, click on your name/organization at the lower left corner of Juicebox, then select **Account Settings > Organization > General**.

![Open Organization settings](/images/help/account/change-organization-name-1.png)

Then, click into the box with the current name of your organization, make any needed changes, and click **Save Changes**.

![Edit the organization name and save](/images/help/account/change-organization-name-2.png)

For a full overview of organization settings, see [Manage Account](/manage-account). If you have any further questions, reach out to us at [support@juicebox.ai](mailto:support@juicebox.ai).
