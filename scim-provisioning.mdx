## Overview

SCIM (System for Cross-domain Identity Management) allows your organization to manage Juicebox users directly through your identity provider, such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, or Google Workspace.

With SCIM, your IT team can automatically add and remove users, manage their roles, and control who has access to Juicebox, without manually updating team members in the platform.

Once SCIM is enabled, your identity provider becomes the source of truth for user access and permissions.

## Before you begin

To set up SCIM, your organization must:

- Be on an eligible **Juicebox Business Plan**.
- Have Single Sign-On (SSO) configured and active. SSO enforcement must also be on.
- Have at least one user who has successfully signed in through SSO.
- Have an IT administrator with access to your organization's identity provider.

<Note>
  SCIM requires SSO. If your organization hasn't configured SSO yet, you'll need to complete that setup before enabling SCIM.
</Note>

## Managing users with SCIM

After SCIM is enabled, user management works a little differently.

### Adding new users

To add a user, assign them to the Juicebox application through your identity provider.

Once provisioned, they can access Juicebox using your organization's SSO login.

SCIM-provisioned users will not receive a separate invitation email from Juicebox.

### Updating user roles

To change a user's role, move them to the appropriate role group in your identity provider. Change the user's role group only, not the group that assigns the Juicebox application.

Roles can no longer be changed directly within Juicebox once SCIM is enforced.

### Removing users

To remove a user's access, deactivate or un-assign them from the Juicebox application in your identity provider.

When access is removed:

- The user is signed out of Juicebox.
- Their active outreach sequences are paused.
- Their AI Agents continue running until reassigned by an administrator.

If the user is added back through your identity provider, their existing Juicebox account is restored. Any paused sequences will need to be resumed manually.

<Note>
  If you're using Okta, suspending a user does not automatically revoke their Juicebox access. You must deactivate the user or un-assign them from the Juicebox application.
</Note>

## Seat limits and billing

When additional licensed users are added through SCIM, Juicebox automatically adjusts your organization's seat limit as needed. Everyone your identity provider assigns is provisioned, even if your organization is over its seat limit.

Juicebox holds the extra seats during the day and bills them once a day:

- **During the day:** If provisioning new users takes your organization over its seat limit, Juicebox holds the additional seats so invitations aren't blocked. Your seat limit and subscription don't change yet, and no email is sent.
- **Nightly at 7 PM Pacific:** Juicebox recounts your licensed users and raises your seat limit to that count, up to a maximum of 1,000 licensed seats. Seats that were added and then freed again before 7 PM are not billed.
- **Invoicing:** Juicebox creates a Stripe draft invoice for the added seats, prorated for the remainder of your term. If your organization has a single card on file, Juicebox charges it automatically about 24 hours later. Otherwise, Juicebox sends the invoice with 30-day payment terms.
- **Confirmation:** Organization administrators receive a **Seat upgrade confirmed** email. You receive at most one seat email per day, and only on days when SCIM added seats.

Removing users never lowers your seat limit. Freed seats are reused by the next user your identity provider assigns.

<Warning>
  Adding licensed users through SCIM may increase your subscription costs. We recommend reviewing your organization's seat requirements before provisioning large groups of users.
</Warning>

## Changing or removing your directory

<Warning>
  Do not delete the directory in the setup portal while SCIM is active. Deleting it locks your organization: all users lose access, and running AI Agents and outreach sequences are paused until Juicebox helps you reconnect a new directory. Contact your Customer Success Manager before making changes to the directory.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I still invite users directly from Juicebox?">
    No. Once SCIM is enabled, all user invitations must be managed through your organization's identity provider.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change a user's role in Juicebox?">
    No. Roles must be managed through your identity provider when SCIM is enabled.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does SCIM require SSO?">
    Yes. Your organization must have an active SSO connection before SCIM can be enabled. Once enabled, all users must sign in using SSO.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will new users receive an invitation email?">
    No. Users provisioned through SCIM can access Juicebox using your organization's SSO login. No separate Juicebox invitation email is sent.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I remove a user?">
    Their access to Juicebox is revoked, and their active sequences are paused. Their AI Agents continue running until reassigned by an administrator.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will adding users affect my subscription?">
    It may. If the number of licensed users exceeds your current seat limit, Juicebox holds the additional seats during the day. At 7 PM Pacific, Juicebox raises your seat limit to your licensed user count and creates an invoice for the added seats. Administrators receive one **Seat upgrade confirmed** email on days when seats were added.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
