Memory is how Juicebox personalizes AI-generated work (searches, criteria, sequences, and Agents) to the way you and your team actually recruit. You can review and edit everything Juicebox has learned from the **Memory** tab in your account settings.

To open it, click your name/organization at the bottom of the left-hand menu, select **Account Settings**, then open the **Memory** tab.

![The Memory tab in Account Settings](/images/memory/memory-tab-location.png)

## Types of memory

Juicebox stores two kinds of memory:

- **Profile and Org Context**: background research about you and your organization, gathered automatically when you sign up.
- **Usage Memory**: preferences and patterns Juicebox picks up from the searches and inputs you run day-to-day.

Both feed into your searches, sequences, and Agents so that results reflect what your team is actually looking for, without you having to repeat yourself in every prompt.

## Profile and Org Context

When you sign up, Juicebox automatically runs deep research online about:

- **You**: your role, background, and other publicly available context.
- **Your organization**: what your company does, and other publicly available context.

This is general background information about you and your org, not recruiting-specific preferences. Juicebox uses it as context for **Search**, **Sequences**, and **Agents**.

### Notifications when memory updates

Whenever Juicebox adds to or updates your Profile and Org Context, you'll get an in-app notification. Open the notification to review what changed.

### Editing memory

You can edit Profile and Org Context at any time from the **Memory** tab:

- Update or remove entries that are out of date or inaccurate.
- Add context Juicebox may have missed (for example, a new business line, a hiring focus, or a preferred candidate profile).

Changes take effect immediately and apply to future searches, sequences, and Agent runs.

### Refreshing Org Context

The **Org Context** narrative can be refreshed on demand from the **Memory** tab, and it also refreshes automatically in a few situations. Every refresh is non-destructive: Juicebox only overwrites the narrative after the new research succeeds. If a run fails, the previous text stays visible so your Org Context is never blank.

Every overwrite (a manual edit, an on-demand refresh, or a scheduled refresh) archives the prior state to a version history on the doc, so admins can see what changed and when.

#### Scheduled refreshes

Organizations on paid plans get a daily scheduled refresh for Org Context narratives whose research is more than about 30 days old. This keeps the org description current without an admin having to remember to click refresh.

- The daily job is capped at 200 organizations per day and is rolled out gradually, so a newly-stale narrative may not refresh on the very first day it becomes eligible.
- If a narrative hasn't been manually edited, the scheduled refresh runs through the standard enrichment worker and overwrites in place.
- If an admin has manually edited the narrative, the scheduled refresh runs fresh research and then reconciles the admin's edits with the new content (see below).

#### Reviewing refreshes that conflict with your edits

When a scheduled refresh finds new information that conflicts with a manual edit, Juicebox doesn't silently overwrite your changes. Instead:

- Clean merges (where the admin's edits and the fresh research don't disagree) apply automatically.
- Real conflicts land as a **pending review** on the Org Context narrative. Admins get a `memory.refreshReview` notification pointing them to the **Memory** tab.
- The pending review opens as a side-by-side dialog showing your current narrative next to the proposed refresh. Choose **Accept** to apply the new version (the prior narrative is archived to version history), or **Reject** to keep your edits.
- Rejecting a review is sticky: Juicebox won't propose the same refresh again until the narrative is edited or refreshed. That lets you mark an edit as authoritative without a scheduled job undoing it the next day.

#### Refresh from network sourcing

When you use [network sourcing](/network-sourcing) and confirm a candidate's company through LinkedIn, Juicebox compares the confirmed company to the one stored in Org Context. If they differ, Org Context is automatically refreshed against the confirmed company so future results reflect the right organization.

Re-confirming the same company doesn't trigger a refresh, and confirmations made by skipping the LinkedIn connection step don't trigger one either (the typed name alone isn't a strong enough signal to reseed org research).

## Usage Memory

Usage Memory captures preferences Juicebox learns from how you actually use the product, including the searches you run, the criteria you add, the profiles you shortlist or hide, and the feedback you give.

Over time, this lets Juicebox anticipate the requirements you tend to care about without you having to spell them out every time.

Usage Memory is maintained automatically in the background. It isn't directly editable from the **Memory** tab, only Profile and Org Context can be reviewed and edited there.

### Criteria

Usage Memory surfaces directly in [criteria](/criteria). When Juicebox adds a criterion based on what it has learned from your past searches, it's labeled **criteria added by memory**.

Hover the memory icon next to a criterion to see the specific memory that influenced it.
